## 2. What data we collect

Through the ordinary use of the platform, we (on behalf of our Customers)
process:

- **Account data**: name, email address, role, login history
- **Business data entered by Customers**: lead and customer names, phone
  numbers, email addresses, property and payment details, employee records,
  vendor details, and other information Customers choose to enter
- **Technical data**: IP address, browser type, access logs, error logs

## 3. Why we collect it

- To provide and operate the platform
- To authenticate users and keep accounts secure
- To respond to support requests
- To improve the product (aggregated, non-identifying usage patterns only)
- To comply with legal obligations
